# Domain glossary

- **Source material**: the text that a reviewer supplies as the basis for questions.
- **Candidate**: an unapproved, editable question with difficulty, topics, explanation, and a verbatim evidence quote.
- **Category**: multiple choice, true/false, or code analysis, with its own candidate count and approval target.
- **Assessment**: the ordered review of three categories, producing five approved questions using a fixed provider and model.
- **Coverage gap**: the provider's report that source material lacks enough information for a category or regeneration.
- **Trainer edition**: approved questions including answers, explanations, and evidence.
- **Student edition**: approved questions without those trainer-only fields.
- **Saved settings**: the committed provider/model selection and presence flags for encrypted keys.
- **Settings draft**: uncommitted dialog edits, discarded when the dialog closes.
